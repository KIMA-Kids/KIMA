Family Coaching — The G.R.O.W.T.H. Program

The G.R.O.W.T.H. program is built on my own six-step process — one step per letter, designed to change how your whole family talks, listens, and grows together.

  • G — Gain understanding. Before anything changes, we get honest about what's actually happening — for your child and for you. Behavior is a message; we learn to read it.
  • R — Take Responsibility. Everyone in the family has a part. No blame — just each person seeing their piece clearly.
  • O — Take Ownership. Responsibility becomes action. Kids and parents each commit to what they'll do differently.
  • W — Wholesome Communication. The family learns to talk so everyone feels heard — including the ability to respectfully disagree.
  • T — Time for Boundaries. A voice doesn't mean control. We set boundaries that hold — explained, not just enforced.
  • H — Honor Values. The family names what it stands for, so the changes have roots long after our work ends.
